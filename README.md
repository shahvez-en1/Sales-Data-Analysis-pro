# 🏪 Sales Data Analysis (Business Insights Project)

## 📌 Project Overview

This project performs *Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA)* on a retail Superstore dataset to uncover valuable business insights related to sales, profit, customer behavior, and regional performance.

The objective of this project is to analyze historical sales data and generate actionable insights that can support data-driven business decisions.

---

## 🎯 Objectives

* Analyze region-wise sales performance
* Identify profitable and loss-making product categories
* Study monthly and yearly sales trends
* Identify top-performing customers
* Detect business improvement opportunities

---

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies Used

* Python
* Pandas
* NumPy
* Matplotlib
* Seaborn
* Jupyter Notebook

## 📊 Exploratory Analysis Performed

### 🔹 Sales Analysis

* Total Sales Calculation
* Region-wise Sales Comparison
* Category-wise Sales Performance

### 🔹 Profit Analysis

* Profit by Category
* Profit by Sub-Category
* Identification of Loss-Making Products

### 🔹 Time Series Analysis

* Monthly Sales Trend
* Year-wise Revenue Growth
* Seasonal Pattern Detection

### 🔹 Customer Analysis

* Top 10 Customers by Revenue
* High-value Customer Identification

### 🔹 Data Visualization

* Bar Charts
* Line Charts
* Heatmaps
* Comparative Analysis Graphs

---

## 📈 Key Business Insights

* Certain regions contribute significantly higher revenue than others
* Some product categories generate high sales but low profit margins
* Clear seasonal sales trends are observed across months
* A small group of customers contributes a major portion of total revenue
